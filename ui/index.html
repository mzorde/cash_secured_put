How these stocks were chosen

Every S&P 500 and Nasdaq-100 name is scanned, and a stock qualifies only when it passes all four independent filters below. Qualifiers are then ranked by put premium yield.

1 Uptrend

Price is at least 15% higher than it was ~378 trading days (about 1.5 years) ago.

2 Valuation & earnings health

Positive trailing earnings, and either a P/E below 100 or cash reserves at least equal to market cap.

3 Cash-secured put premium

On the ~30-day expiry, the put nearest 0.30 delta yields at least 2.5% (premium ÷ strike).

4 Earnings consistency

At least 4 reported quarters, an unbroken quarterly cadence, and no 3 consecutive misses.

Qualifiers are ordered by premium yield (highest first); ties break alphabetically by ticker. Each list is capped at the top 20.
